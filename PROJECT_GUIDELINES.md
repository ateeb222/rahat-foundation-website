# Rahat Social Impact Foundation Website Guidelines

## Organization Overview
Rahat Social Impact Foundation is a healthcare-focused NGO dedicated to improving patient access, hospital infrastructure, digital health transformation, patient support, and community healthcare outreach. The website should present Rahat as a trusted healthcare institution supported by community partners, volunteers, and donors.

## Technology Stack
- Next.js 15
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS
- Vercel for deployment
- Sanity CMS for content management
- Razorpay for donation processing

## Program Definitions
- Mobility & Patient Access: Improving physical access for patients through wheelchair distribution, mobility aids, and patient mobility support.
- Hospital Support Infrastructure: Strengthening hospital capacity with stretchers, patient support equipment, facility upgrades, and infrastructure support.
- Digital Health Transformation: Enabling hospital digitization with patient records, queue management, online payment systems, computers, printers, and healthcare IT solutions.
- Patient Support & Navigation: Helping patients navigate the healthcare system, access services, and receive compassionate support throughout their journey.
- Community Healthcare Outreach: Extending preventive, primary, and awareness-driven healthcare services into local communities.

## Mobile-First Requirement
The website must be designed and built with a mobile-first mindset. All layouts, navigation, interactions, and content hierarchy should prioritize small screens first, then scale gracefully to larger viewports.

## Trust and Transparency Principles
- Present Rahat as a healthcare institution rooted in accountability and community support.
- Surface clear trust signals such as partner hospitals, certifications, impact metrics, and transparent governance details.
- Provide easy access to registration details, financial reports, governance documents, and policies.
- Use honest, factual language and avoid overtly fundraising-first messaging.

## Donation Philosophy
- Donations should be framed in terms of ongoing healthcare impact rather than one-time charity appeals.
- Encourage recurring support alongside one-time giving to build sustainable program funding.
- Make the donation experience secure, straightforward, and clearly connected to healthcare outcomes.
- Highlight Razorpay as the trusted payments partner.
